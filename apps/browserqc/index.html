Technical log

BrowserQC

Automated MRI quality control, entirely in your browser — no upload, no server. Drag in a NIfTI image (or a folder of DICOM files) and it runs automatically.

  • Segmentation — a brainchop deep-learning model (MindGrab) parcellates the brain into 17 gray/white-matter and subcortical regions, shown as a colour overlay on your scan (adjust with the Opacity slider)
  • Quality metrics — niimath computes MRIQC-style image-quality metrics (CJV, CNR, SNR, WM2MAX, EFC, tissue composition) from the scan and its segmentation

Rendering uses NiiVue; DICOM import uses dcm2niix.

The image-quality metrics follow MRIQC: Esteban et al. (2017) MRIQC: Advancing the automatic prediction of image quality in MRI from unseen sites. PLoS One 12(9):e0184661. PMID: 28945803. Values are an MRIQC-style approximation, not MRIQC normative values.

How to cite BrowserQC

Cite the MRIQC methods paper (Esteban et al., 2017) and the NiiVue, niimath, dcm2niix, and MindGrab software used in your analysis.

Privacy

BrowserQC processes scans locally in your browser. It does not upload your images, segmentations, or quality reports.
